How it works

Have it ready for when you get a nosebleed!

Prepare

  1. 1

    Step 1: Load the cartridge

    Drop the cartridge into the main body and screw on the nose head.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Lock the stem

    Insert the cotton from the bottom. Then insert the stem, twist it to the right, and pull it down. Instaclot is now locked.

Use

  1. 3

    Step 3: Place, push, pinch and hold

    When you get a nosebleed, push the stem up, twist the stem to the left, insert the nose head into your nose, and push the stem up. Gently pinch the nose to release the constricting element from the cotton, and wait 5 minutes before removing the cotton.

Clean

  1. 4

    Step 4: Rinse all three parts

    Wash everything off and repeat steps 1 and 2 so you are prepared for your next nosebleed.

What it solves

  • Most common causes:
  • Dry air
  • Hits to the face
  • Picking/rubbing
  • Allergies
  • Blood thinners
